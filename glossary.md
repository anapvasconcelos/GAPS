# GAPS Glossary


## Anonymized data
Anonymized data are data rendered anonymous in such a way that the data subject is no longer identifiable. <!-- OpenAIRE -->



## Citation
Citation is a formal reference to a research work, such as a paper, dataset, or software. It allows others to identify, locate, and access the original source. Citations usually appear in the text and are detailed in a reference list. In some cases, they may also point to specific parts of a work, such as a page, figure, or subset of data. <!-- DataCitationSynthesisGroup2014 -->



## Data
Data are any materials collected or generated during research that can support a scientific argument. This includes, for example, measurements, survey responses, interviews, logs, images, or any other information produced by humans or machines.  <!-- DataCitationSynthesisGroup2014 -->



## Dataset
A dataset is an organized collection of data, often accompanied by documentation and metadata, that represents the data used or produced in a study. It includes recorded information, regardless of the form or medium on which it may be recorded, such as writings, films, sound recordings, designs, or other graphic representations, procedural manuals, forms, diagrams, work flow, charts, equipment descriptions, data files, data processing or computer programs (software), statistical records, and other research data.  <!-- DataCitationSynthesisGroup2014 -->



## Identifier
An identifier is a character string used to refer to an object, such as a dataset, file, or person. It helps distinguish that object from others. <!-- DataCitationSynthesisGroup2014 -->



## Metadata
Metadata are structured information that describe a digital object, including details such as authorship, creation date, content, format, and processing steps. <!-- DataCitationSynthesisGroup2014 -->



## Persistent identifier (PID)
A persistent identifier is a long-lasting identifier that remains the same over time, even if the object is moved or renamed. It allows the object to be reliably found and cited in the future. <!-- DataCitationSynthesisGroup2014 -->



## Personal data
Personal data means any information relating to a natural person who is either identified or who could be identifiable by that data (e.g., by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, online identifier or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural or social identity of that person). <!-- OpenAIRE -->



## Version
A version is a specific state of a dataset or artifact at a given point in time, usually identified by a number or date, allowing changes to be tracked and previous states to be reproduced. <!-- DataCitationSynthesisGroup2014 -->
