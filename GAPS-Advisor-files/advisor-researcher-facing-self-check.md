# Researcher-facing self-check

Used by researchers to perform a final, high-level check of their artifact before sharing.


### Purpose and scope

- [ ] Does the artifact have a clear purpose?

- [ ] Does the artifact contain all materials necessary for its stated purpose?

- [ ] Have unnecessary, obsolete, temporary, or unrelated files been removed?


### Organization and documentation

- [ ] Is the repository organized?

- [ ] Are dependencies documented?

- [ ] Are expected inputs and outputs clearly documented?

- [ ] Is the relationship between inputs, processing steps, and outputs clear?

- [ ] Can another researcher install the artifact?

- [ ] Can another researcher execute the artifact?

- [ ] Are outputs linked to the paper?

- [ ] Are manual and automated steps clearly distinguished?


### Data and transparency

- [ ] Is the provenance of the data documented, when applicable?

- [ ] Are raw, processed, and derived data clearly distinguished, when applicable?

- [ ] Are non-shared components explained?

- [ ] Has sensitive information been removed or appropriately restricted?


### Reproducibility and validation

- [ ] Has the artifact been tested outside the development environment?

- [ ] Has the artifact been tested by someone unfamiliar with the research, when feasible?

- [ ] Is the artifact consistent with the paper and its documentation?

- [ ] Are known limitations documented?


### Publication and preservation

- [ ] Is the artifact version clearly identified?

- [ ] Does the artifact have a persistent identifier?

- [ ] Is the license clear?

- [ ] Is citation information provided?

- [ ] Is sufficient metadata provided to identify and locate the artifact?

- [ ] Is the artifact preserved in an appropriate repository?

- [ ] Have all links, references, and embedded resources been checked?


### Final release

- [ ] Are external dependencies and resources clearly identified, and are the conditions required to access them documented?

- [ ] Is the final version of the artifact clean and ready for sharing?
