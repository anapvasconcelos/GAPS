# Where should I start?

Research artifact preparation is an ongoing process, from planning and organizing research materials to documenting, validating, publishing, and preserving them. You do not need to read all GAPS modules in sequence. Start with the task you are working on and consult the modules most relevant to your current needs.

## Choose your starting point

| If you want to... | Start with... |
|---|---|
| Understand what research artifacts are and why they matter | [Module 1 - Foundations](/GAPS-Material-files/M1.md) |
| Plan artifact preparation from the beginning of your research and organize files, data, and repositories | [Module 2 - Planning and artifact organization](/GAPS-Material-files/M2.md) |
| Write or improve your README, document your artifact, or explain how it relates to your paper | [Module 3 - Documentation](/GAPS-Material-files/M3.md) |
| Specify dependencies, configure an execution environment, or automate a research workflow | [Module 4 - Environments, dependencies, and automation](/GAPS-Material-files/M4.md) |
| Prepare data for sharing while addressing privacy, anonymization, consent, or ethical and legal constraints | [Module 5 - Data, privacy, and ethics](/GAPS-Material-files/M5.md) |
| Test whether your artifact is complete, usable, and consistent with your paper before release | [Module 6 - Validation and pre-release preparation](/GAPS-Material-files/M6.md) |
| Choose a repository, package and publish an artifact, select a license, or prepare citation metadata and a Data Availability Statement | [Module 7 - Publishing, licensing, and citation](/GAPS-Material-files/M7.md) |
| Preserve an artifact, manage versions, document changes, or maintain it after publication | [Module 8 - Preservation, versioning, and maintenance](/GAPS-Material-files/M8.md) |
| Explain how others can reuse or extend your artifact beyond the original study | [Module 9 - Reusability and extension](/GAPS-Material-files/M9.md) |
| Prepare an artifact for submission to an evaluation process or understand what reviewers may assess | [Module 10 - Artifact evaluation and submission](/GAPS-Material-files/M10.md) |

## Working on more than one task?

Artifact preparation involves interconnected activities. You may need to consult multiple modules to address a single task. For example:

- **Preparing an artifact for submission:** Start with Module 6 to validate the artifact, then consult Module 7 for publication requirements and Module 10 for the target venue's artifact evaluation process.
- **Sharing research data involving human participants:** Start with Module 5 to assess ethical, legal, and privacy constraints. Consult Modules 2 and 3 for data organization and documentation, and Module 7 for publication and access conditions.
- **Making an artifact reproducible:** Start with Module 4 for environments and automation, Module 3 for execution instructions, and Module 6 for independent validation.
- **Improving an existing artifact:** Use the [Researcher-facing Self-check](/resources/researcher-facing-self-check.md) to identify aspects that may need attention, then consult the corresponding modules for guidance.

## Looking for a quick reference?

You can also use the complementary resources:

- **[README Template](/resources/template-README.md):** A starting point for documenting a research artifact.
- **[Researcher-facing Self-check](/resources/researcher-facing-self-check.md):** A checklist to help assess artifact readiness before sharing or submission.
- **[GAPS structure](/resources/structure.md):** An overview of how the modules and complementary resources are organized.
- **[GAPS Advisor](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/17518b1f-e320-48be-a9c2-0edee13c6737):** An interactive advisor that provides guidance based on GAPS recommendations and the researcher's current situation.

> [!TIP]
> You do not need to apply every recommendation to every artifact. The appropriate practices depend on the artifact's nature, intended use, research context, and any ethical, legal, or technical constraints. Use GAPS to identify and address the requirements relevant to your situation.
