# Researcher-facing self-check

Used by researchers to perform a final, high-level check of their artifact before sharing.


### Purpose and scope

<input type="checkbox"> Does the artifact have a clear purpose?

<input type="checkbox"> Does the artifact contain all materials necessary for its stated purpose?

<input type="checkbox"> Have unnecessary, obsolete, temporary, or unrelated files been removed?


### Organization and documentation

<input type="checkbox"> Is the repository organized?

<input type="checkbox"> Are dependencies documented?

<input type="checkbox"> Are expected inputs and outputs clearly documented?

<input type="checkbox"> Is the relationship between inputs, processing steps, and outputs clear?

<input type="checkbox"> Can another researcher install the artifact?

<input type="checkbox"> Can another researcher execute the artifact?

<input type="checkbox"> Are outputs linked to the paper?

<input type="checkbox"> Are manual and automated steps clearly distinguished?


### Data and transparency

<input type="checkbox"> Is the provenance of the data documented, when applicable?

<input type="checkbox"> Are raw, processed, and derived data clearly distinguished, when applicable?

<input type="checkbox"> Are non-shared components explained?

<input type="checkbox"> Has sensitive information been removed or appropriately restricted?


### Reproducibility and validation

<input type="checkbox"> Has the artifact been tested outside the development environment?

<input type="checkbox"> Has the artifact been tested by someone unfamiliar with the research, when feasible?

<input type="checkbox"> Is the artifact consistent with the paper and its documentation?

<input type="checkbox"> Are known limitations documented?


### Publication and preservation

<input type="checkbox"> Is the artifact version clearly identified?

<input type="checkbox"> Does the artifact have a persistent identifier?

<input type="checkbox"> Is the license clear?

<input type="checkbox"> Is citation information provided?

<input type="checkbox"> Is sufficient metadata provided to identify and locate the artifact?

<input type="checkbox"> Is the artifact preserved in an appropriate repository?

<input type="checkbox"> Have all links, references, and embedded resources been checked?


### Final release

<input type="checkbox"> Are external dependencies and resources clearly identified, and are the conditions required to access them documented?

<input type="checkbox"> Is the final version of the artifact clean and ready for sharing?
